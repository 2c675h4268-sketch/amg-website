Buy stocks such as Warner Music, Morgan Stanley says

Source: CNBC  ·  September 20, 2026

Morgan Stanley analysts identified SpaceX among a list of stocks considered attractive for investors looking to buy on weakness. The Wall Street bank also highlighted SiTime and EchoStar alongside SpaceX in its latest recommendations. The endorsement underscores continued Wall Street confidence in the commercial space sector.

The Atomic take

Morgan Stanley naming SpaceX in a buy-on-weakness list is notable precisely because SpaceX remains private, so most retail investors have no direct way to act on the call without going through secondary markets or funds with exposure. Pairing it with EchoStar, which controls valuable spectrum, and SiTime, whose precision timing chips matter for satellites, suggests the bank is betting on the plumbing of commercial space rather than just launch. Watch whether this pressure builds toward an eventual Starlink IPO that would finally give public buyers a way in.
