Jensen Huang: AI CEOs Have 'Ulterior Reasons' in Doomsday Warnings

Source: Business Insider  ·  September 20, 2026

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang accused frontier AI lab leaders of being disingenuous in their apocalyptic warnings, arguing that current regulations are sufficient to ensure AI safety while remaining profitable.

The Atomic take

Huang's dismissal reflects a fundamental split between hardware suppliers who profit from AI proliferation and frontier labs whose safety rhetoric doubles as a case for regulatory moats that would entrench incumbents. His claim that existing rules suffice conveniently aligns with Nvidia's interest in keeping chip demand unconstrained by new compliance burdens. Watch whether other infrastructure players echo this position and how it plays into the coming fights over export controls and any federal AI safety framework.
