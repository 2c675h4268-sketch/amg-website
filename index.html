This week's intelligence brief

Five forces shaping where humanity goes next: artificial intelligence, robotics, space, longevity, and the energy and compute powering them. A two-minute investment thesis on each, every weekday.

Artificial Intelligence

The real contest in AI moves from the labs to Washington: the edge shifts from who trains the best model to who shapes the rules and earns public trust.

Robotics

Humanoids move from stage demos to real deployment orders, while the companies selling the shared brains and chips win no matter who builds the body.

Space

Space turns into paid infrastructure as defense dollars become the most reliable paycheck and a few vertically integrated owners capture the money.

Longevity

Approved therapies beat promising pipelines: money flows to the drugmakers who get across the FDA finish line and can actually sell.

Energy & Compute

Power and permits, not chips, decide AI's winners, as electricity and the permission to build become the real bottleneck.
